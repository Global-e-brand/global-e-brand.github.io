Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GEB ("we", "us", "the platform") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect a third-party account such as Pinterest to GEB and use the scheduling and reporting features the platform provides.

1. What we collect

When you connect a Pinterest business account to GEB, we collect and store only the data needed to operate the features you requested:

  • Your Pinterest account identifier and account name.
  • The list of boards owned by your connected account.
  • The image and caption content you upload to GEB and choose to publish as Pins.
  • Pin performance metrics returned by Pinterest for Pins published through GEB (impressions, saves, outbound clicks).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Pinterest, used to call the Pinterest API on your behalf.

2. How we use it

The data above is used only to:

  • Display your boards inside GEB so you can choose where to publish.
  • Publish the Pins you scheduled, at the time you scheduled them.
  • Show you the performance of Pins GEB published for you.

We do not use this data for advertising, profiling of other Pinterest users, or any purpose unrelated to the features you actively use.

3. How we store it

Account data and OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest in our managed database (Supabase, hosted on AWS). Tokens are accessed only by GEB server-side functions to call the Pinterest API on your behalf.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share your Pinterest data with third parties. The only external party that receives any of this data is Pinterest itself, when we make API calls on your behalf to perform actions you have requested (for example, publishing a Pin you scheduled).

5. Your rights and controls

  • Disconnect at any time. You can disconnect your Pinterest account from inside GEB. You can also revoke GEB's access directly from your Pinterest account security settings, which immediately invalidates the tokens we hold.
  • Deletion. When you disconnect, the OAuth tokens and stored Pinterest account metadata are deleted. You can request deletion of any remaining historical data (such as published Pin records and metrics) by emailing the address below.
  • Access. You can request a copy of the data GEB holds about your connected account by emailing the address below.

6. Data retention

OAuth tokens and account metadata are retained while your Pinterest account is connected to GEB. Pin records and performance metrics are retained for as long as you keep your GEB account, so that historical reporting remains available to you, and are deleted on request.

7. Security

All traffic between GEB, Pinterest, and your browser uses HTTPS. Tokens and user data are encrypted at rest. Access to production systems is limited to authorised GEB engineering personnel.

8. Children

GEB is intended for business users and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, data access requests, or deletion requests, contact baquir@an-associates.com.
